Rollwave — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Rollwave ("the app") is a free mobile game published by Milan Kinzy ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we collect

The app itself does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your scores and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect device information such as your IP address (used to derive a coarse location), device model and operating system, app usage and ad interaction data, and — only if you grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt — your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you decline, you still receive ads, but they are not personalized using that identifier.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking lets you allow or revoke tracking for Rollwave at any time.
  • Ad personalization (EEA/UK and applicable US states): open Settings inside the app and tap "Ad privacy options" to change your consent choices.
  • Personalized ads from Google: manage at adssettings.google.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and security

We do not operate servers that receive data from the app. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
