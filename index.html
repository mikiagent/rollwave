Rollwave

Outrun the dark. A steep, endless downhill ball game for iPhone: hold the screen to steer and weigh the ball down, hit boost pads, fly off giant ramps between islands, flick in the air for tricks, grab coins and sky hoops — and stay ahead of the darkness chasing you.

How to play

  • Touch and hold to make the ball heavy and grip the slope. Drag left or right to steer.
  • Swipe up for a super push (recharges in 7 seconds). Swipe down to brake.
  • Air time scores: every tenth of a second off a ramp or crest counts.
  • In the air, swipe up or down for flips and left or right for spins. Each trick also nudges you that way.
  • Coins on the ground (+10), hoops in the sky (+300), boost pads for speed.
  • Island breaks: the track narrows into a boosted run-up and a giant ramp launches you across. You always make it — just keep your speed.
  • The dark follows you. If it catches you, the run ends.
